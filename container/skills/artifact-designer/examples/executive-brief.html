When two repeats would make sense

Two 60-minute repeats could accommodate conflicting schedules. With the same 45-minute fixed agenda in each repeat, practice falls to 15 minutes per attendee. That tradeoff is reasonable only if attendance would otherwise be lost.

Unknown: participants’ availability. The sample supplies no evidence of a scheduling conflict.

Assumptions and arithmetic

All counts and durations here are invented to demonstrate a brief. One facilitator is assumed available for either 90 minutes or two 60-minute repeats. Fixed agenda time is 15 + 10 + 20 = 45 minutes per session. The calculation excludes breaks, setup and transitions; add those before using it for a real event.
