The model is deliberately simple

Items/week = floor(hours × 60 ÷ minutes/item)
Weeks = ceil(items ÷ items/week)

“Floor” rounds down to whole items; “ceil” rounds up to whole weeks. Total reading time = items × minutes per item ÷ 60.

Assumes steady time and equal effort. It excludes new arrivals, interruptions and breaks. For a real plan, leave room for those before choosing a weekly commitment.
