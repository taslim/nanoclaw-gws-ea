How to read the comparison

  • Requirements are assumed for this exercise: 24 people and 90 minutes together.
  • Capacity is seated capacity. Time is one uninterrupted block, in minutes.
  • Each observation cites a sample note and its date; none is a real inspection record.

What a real decision still needs

Confirm access needs with participants, inspect routes and facilities, and check availability. A level entrance alone does not establish that the whole event is accessible.

Checklist reference: W3C accessible events guidance. This reference informs what to ask, not the invented room findings.
